**A command that fails leaves nothing in the fakes, as a real adapter rolls back.** With `createTestBuses` of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` and the test application of `@ddd-ts-kit/di`, a command whose handler, before-commit handler or commit step failed used to keep in the fakes what it had saved, and a spec could assert a state no database would leave. `InMemoryUnitOfWork` now rolls back: when its outermost `run` rejects, the stores in memory undo what the work wrote, newest first, and the aggregates it saved are given back at the version they were loaded at, with their events, as on the four ORM. After a failed command a spec sees in `InMemoryRepository` what it held before, removals included; nothing of it in `InMemoryOutboxStore`, `InMemoryReadModelStore` (and `InMemoryReadModelTable`), `InMemoryProcessStore`, `InMemoryCheckpointStore`, `InMemorySnapshotStore` or `InMemoryEventStore` (whose positions leave a gap, as a database sequence does); the mark of `InMemoryProcessedEventStore` given back; and, as before, no domain event of the command reached an `after-commit` handler and no job of `InMemoryJobStore` was added. The undo of a key leaves what a command running alongside wrote since. `unitOfWorkContract` now holds `InMemoryUnitOfWork`, an `InMemoryRepository` and an `InMemoryOutboxStore` to the same contract as the ORM. `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel` exports what the fakes are built on, for a fake of your own: `onRollback(undo)`, which hands an undo to the unit of work in progress, and `RollbackMap`, a Map whose `set` and `delete` are undone that way. The in-memory outbox wakes a relay once the unit of work committed, no longer at the append.

`InMemoryEventBus.published` is not rolled back: the bus stands for a broker, and no transaction covers a broker. In a generated application with `--events outbox` a publisher writes to the outbox, which rolls back with the command; the test buses and the test application hand the publisher the bus itself, so in a spec what a handler published straight on the bus stays in `publishedEvents()` after a failure.

To move: a spec that asserted a save, a row or an outbox record left by a command that failed now sees it gone: assert what the database would hold, the state before the command.
To move: `unitOfWorkOver(transaction, { rollsBack: false })` no longer compiles: the option is gone, every unit of work gives the aggregates back. A unit of work of your own over a store that undoes nothing hands its undos to `onRollback`, or keeps its rows in a `RollbackMap`.
To move: a fake of your own over a plain `Map`, saved again with the same instance after a failed command, now refuses it with a `ConcurrencyError` (the aggregate is given back, the Map kept the save): keep its rows in a `RollbackMap` of `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel`, or extend `InMemoryRepository`.
