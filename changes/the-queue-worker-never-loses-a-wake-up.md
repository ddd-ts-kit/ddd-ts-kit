**A queue of the `QueueWorker` no longer stops when an attempt ends while its depth is counted.** A worker given `metrics` asks the store for the depth of a queue after it claimed; an attempt that ended during that count asked the queue to claim again and was not heard, so with the default concurrency of 1 the queue ran one job and no other, with no timer left to wake it. A wake-up that comes while a queue fills is now kept, and the queue fills once more after (#900).
