**A key added through `redact` is hidden whatever its case, by every logger that redacts.** `createPinoLogger({ redact: ['iban'] })` writes `[redacted]` for `iban`, `IBAN` and `Iban` alike, at the top of a line and one level below, in the fields of a line, of a child and of `fields`, as `createWinstonLogger` does. The three redacting loggers ask one function of `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel`, `hiddenKeys(added)`, whether a field name is hidden: one of `CREDENTIAL_KEYS` or an added name, without case. `StdoutLogger` of `@ddd-ts-kit/aspects` takes `redact` too. Around a pino instance of your own, `PinoLogger` and `correlationMixin` take the list given to `credentialRedaction`: `new PinoLogger(instance, { redact })`, `correlationMixin(fields, redact)`. `loggerContract` of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` proves it for a logger declared with `redacts: true`, whose `createLogger` then receives `redact`.

**`Logger.flush()` ends nothing, on every logger of the kit.** The port says so now: an entry logged after a flush is written like the others, and a later flush waits for it in turn. `WinstonLogger.flush()` resolves once winston has handed each of its transports every line logged so far, ten seconds at most for a transport that does not answer, and leaves the logger open, as `PinoLogger`, `StdoutLogger` and `InMemoryLogger` do. Ending is a verb of its own, `WinstonLogger.end()`: it ends the winston logger, resolves once each transport has finished, and is final. `loggerContract` holds every logger to an entry logged after a flush being written.

To move: nothing for a logger on the console, which is what `init --logger winston` generates. A project whose winston transports still hold lines they took (the `File` transport, until its file is closed) and that leaves the process by itself after `app.stop()` (`process.exit()`) calls `await logger.end()` first, on the `WinstonLogger` its composition root built: `flush()` no longer closes the file. A `Logger` of your own that dropped what was logged after a flush now writes it, or fails the flush case of `loggerContract`.
