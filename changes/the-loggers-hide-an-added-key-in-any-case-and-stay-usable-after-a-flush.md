**A key added through `redact` is hidden whatever its case, by every logger that redacts.** `createPinoLogger({ redact: ['iban'] })` writes `[redacted]` for `iban`, `IBAN` and `Iban` alike, at the top of a line and one level below, in the fields of a line, of a child and of `fields`, as `createWinstonLogger` does. The three redacting loggers ask one function of `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel`, `hiddenKeys(added)`, whether a field name is hidden: one of `CREDENTIAL_KEYS` or an added name, without case. `StdoutLogger` of `@ddd-ts-kit/aspects` takes `redact` too. Around a pino instance of your own, `PinoLogger` and `correlationMixin` take the list given to `credentialRedaction`: `new PinoLogger(instance, { redact })`, `correlationMixin(fields, redact)`. `loggerContract` of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` proves it for a logger declared with `redacts: true`, whose `createLogger` then receives `redact`.
