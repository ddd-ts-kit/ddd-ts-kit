**A circuit only listens to the calls its current state let in.** A slow call let in while the circuit was closed, and that answered once it was half-open, closed it while the trial call was still running: traffic went back to a dependency that was still down. The same call failing late opened the circuit again and started `resetAfterMs` over, and a failure of a call let in before the circuit opened counted in the circuit closed since. The outcome of a call now goes to the state that let the call in, and decides nothing once the circuit left that state: only a trial call closes or reopens a half-open circuit. The options, `CircuitOpenError` and the transitions `onStateChange` reports do not change (#903).
