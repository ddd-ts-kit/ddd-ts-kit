**An outbox relay started again before its `stop()` resolved keeps one loop.** `OutboxRelay.start()` called while the last tick of a `stop()` that was not awaited is still in progress (a hot reload, a hand-over of leadership) started a second loop beside the first, for ever: two ticks ran at once and the store was claimed twice per interval. It now waits for that tick and runs its first tick after it, so ticks never overlap (#902).
