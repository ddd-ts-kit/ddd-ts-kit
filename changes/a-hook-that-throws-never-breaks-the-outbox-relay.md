**An `onFailure` hook that throws no longer breaks the outbox relay.** `OutboxRelay` drops what its `onFailure` hook throws: a tick goes on with the other records of its batch, where it stopped after the first failed publication, and a started relay no longer leaves an unhandled rejection at each tick, which ended the process under the default policy of Node. `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel` gains `safely(observe)`, which runs a hook, a logger or a meter and keeps its failure to itself; the hooks of the four brokers, the recorders of the scheduler and of the job queue, and the aspects are written with it, with no change of behaviour (#902).
