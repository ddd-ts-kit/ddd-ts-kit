**The guard of the plugin keeps what a worker changed outside its scope in three steps, with no change of behaviour.** `plugin/hooks/held-changes.mjs` notes the state before code the guard cannot read (`note`), compares it at the next call (`settle`, which answers whether the worker is clear, held, or not compared yet, with the reason that refuses the call and what to tell it) and drops the note when a new run of a builder starts (`release`); these three alone write the two state files of a worker, and the entry of the guard alone says what goes with a decision. No rule, no message and no decision changes. A project has nothing to do.
