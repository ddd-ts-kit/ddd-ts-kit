**RabbitMQ's `publish`, `assertTopology` and `close()` never wait for ever on a broker that stopped answering: `publishTimeoutMs` bounds them, the opening of the connection included.** A broker that accepted the connection and never answered AMQP, never opened a channel or never answered a declaration held `publish` and `assertTopology` for ever, and held the connection too: the bus never tried again, so it stayed stuck once the broker was back. `publish` now settles within `publishTimeoutMs` for the whole call, not per step, rejecting with `BrokerUnavailableError` of reason `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` (as Kafka does, and as `replayDead` does since #923) when it was held up before sending, and with `PUBLISH_NOT_CONFIRMED` as before when the confirmation did not come. `assertTopology` settles within the same bound however many declarations it makes. An attempt to open the connection, and the start of a subscription, that the broker does not answer in time fail, are reported to `onFailure` as `REQUEST_TIMEOUT`, and are retried with the delays of `reconnect`. A connection or a channel the broker opens after its timeout, the confirm channel of `replayDead` among them, is closed when it arrives, and `close()` no longer waits for the broker to answer the close of the connection.
