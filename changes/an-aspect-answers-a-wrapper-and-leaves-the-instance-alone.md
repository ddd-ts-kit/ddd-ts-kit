**An aspect answers a wrapper and leaves the instance it is given alone.** `aspect(instance)` answered the instance itself with its methods replaced: two aspects put around one instance were one object carrying both advices, and whoever had kept the instance was advised too. An aspect now answers a wrapper of the same class, a proxy in front of the instance, as it already did for a frozen one: `instanceof` and `private` members keep working, a method runs with the wrapper as `this`, a field read or assigned through the wrapper is the field of the instance, and nothing is added to the instance. An aspect that advises no method still answers the instance itself, and a method decorator of `@ddd-ts-kit/aspects/decorators` still wraps the method of the instances of its own class. The bindings of a container, `aspects(...)`, `ownPolicy` and `defaultPolicy` of a generated project already keep what an aspect answers and need nothing (#903).

To move: keep what the aspect answers (`const rates = policy(new HttpRates())`) where the code applied an aspect and went on with the instance it had. A class wrapped by an aspect that keeps its state in hard-private members (`#name`) throws a `TypeError` at its first call, since such a member is not reached through a proxy: write `private name`, which `clean/no-hash-private` of `@ddd-ts-kit/eslint-plugin` does.
