**A scheduled job runs its next occurrence when the lock of the previous one cannot be released.** A `singleInstance` job keeps the lock of an occurrence until the next one, and lets it go then. When that release failed, a lock server that did not answer for instance, the next occurrence was not run: it was recorded `failed` with `JOB_LOCK_UNAVAILABLE`, although its own lock could be taken. The release is now at best: a lock that cannot be released ends at its time to live, as it would have had the process stopped, and the next occurrence takes its own lock (#900).
