**A record held for order is postponed, not failed: `outbox_failed` counts failures only.** Under `ordering: 'per-key'`, the relay stored a record that waits behind a failed one of its key through `markFailed`, with the code `OUTBOX_HELD_FOR_ORDER`: `stats().failed` and the gauge `outbox_failed` counted it, so one failed publication on a key of three records read as three failures. The `OutboxStore` port gains `postpone(id, until)`, which puts a claimed record back until `until` untried: the claim is not counted as an attempt, the code of its last failure stays, and a record that is published, dead or was never claimed is left alone. The relay holds a record back with it, and no longer keeps in memory how many claims each record spent waiting. The in-memory store and the stores of Knex, Drizzle, Prisma and TypeORM implement it, and `outboxStoreContract` of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` gains three cases that prove it, and one more assertion: a claimed record carries the end of its lease as `nextAttemptAt` (#902).

To move: an `OutboxStore` of your own implements `postpone(id, until)`: on a pending record with at least one attempt, one attempt less and `nextAttemptAt` set to `until`; `outboxStoreContract` says the rest. `HELD_FOR_ORDER` is no longer exported by `@ddd-ts-kit/events`, and no record carries `OUTBOX_HELD_FOR_ORDER` as its `lastErrorCode` any more: drop the filter of an alert or of a query that left it out. A fake outbox handed to `unitOfWorkContract` or `eventStoreUnitOfWorkContract` returns `nextAttemptAt` with each record of `claimBatch`. A Prisma client fits the delegate of `PrismaOutboxStore` as before.
