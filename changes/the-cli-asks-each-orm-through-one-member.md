**Inside the CLI, each ORM is one member that answers what the commands ask of it.** Nothing changes in what `init`, `generate`, `merge-trunk`, `verify-merge` and `db:generate` write or say. What used to be a comparison on the name of the ORM in some forty files is now asked of `ORM_MEMBERS` (`packages/cli/src/orm`): how the migrations of the ORM follow those of a trunk (renamed after its last one by the migration tool of knex, Prisma or TypeORM, generated again under drizzle-kit), where the tables of a context are declared and what the schema index says of their names, and how the generated code reaches the database. A capability an ORM does not have is absent from its member, and the compiler lists what a new member has to answer.
