**RabbitMQ's `replayDead` bounds every wait with `publishTimeoutMs`, not only the confirmation of its copies.** On a broker that stopped answering, opening the confirm channel of the replay and taking the next message of the dead queue (`get`) waited for ever, so the replay never returned. Both requests now reject with `BrokerUnavailableError` of reason `REQUEST_TIMEOUT`, as a request of the Kafka bus does, when the broker has not answered within `publishTimeoutMs`; the message stays in the dead queue, and the replay can be run again once the broker answers (#901).
