**A tick of the outbox relay fails with its first error.** When the store failed in the middle of a batch, `OutboxRelay.tick()` marked what had gone out in a `finally`: a store that could not mark it either replaced the error of the tick with its own, and the first failure was never seen. The tick now rejects with its first error, and the publications it could not mark are told to `onFailure` as `{ kind: 'tick', code }` (#902).
